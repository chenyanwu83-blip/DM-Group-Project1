## 1. 图 D1 `fig_d_sick_roc_pr` — sick ROC + PR 叠加（plain vs SMOTE vs cost）

**取数**：`results/preds/sick_rf_{plain,smote,cost}_v1_run1_cv10_preds.csv` 汇池折外预测（n=3,772），`figures/data/fig_d_sick_roc_pr_summary.csv`。

| strategy | AUC | AP |
|---|---|---|
| plain | 0.9973 | 0.9656 |
| smote | 0.9977 | 0.9671 |
| cost | 0.9972 | 0.9646 |

三策略曲线几乎重合（AUC 差 5e-4 量级）——这不是绘图问题而是 sick 侧真实结构：判别秩几乎不受策略影响，策略差异全部体现在**操作点**移动（阈值/代价改变混淆矩阵而非分数排序，D4 §6 已记 threshold AUC 与 plain 逐位相同）。选 RF 是 sick 主矩阵冠军族（rf_smote 为冠军）。

**图注定稿（供报告直接引用）：**

> **Figure D1.** ROC (a) and Precision–Recall (b) curves on the sick dataset for the Random Forest classifier under the plain, SMOTE, and cost-sensitive strategies, computed from the pooled out-of-fold predictions of the 10-fold cross-validation protocol (all 3,772 instances; positive class = sick). Dotted lines mark the chance level (a) and the positive-class prevalence 231/3,772 = 0.061 (b). The three curves nearly coincide (AUC 0.997–0.998): ranking quality is essentially strategy-invariant, and the strategy effects reported in Table (D4) operate on the operating point rather than on the score ranking.

## 2. 图 D2 `fig_d_strategy_gain` — 策略增益 × 不平衡度（ΔF1 / ΔRecall）

**取数**：`results/cross_dataset_comparison_v1.csv`（D6；源头 C5 12 个预指定 strategy-vs-plain 配对检验，NB 校正 t），4 模型均值，`figures/data/fig_d_strategy_gain.csv`。

| dataset（neg:pos） | 策略 | ΔF1 | ΔRecall |
|---|---|---|---|
| credit-g（2.3:1） | smote | +0.070 | +0.169 |
| credit-g | cost | +0.114 | **+0.424** |
| credit-g | threshold | +0.122 | +0.348 |
| sick（15.3:1） | smote | **−0.062** | +0.213 |
| sick | cost | +0.001 | +0.001 |
| sick | threshold | +0.024 | +0.096 |

图即 D6 两大发现的可视化：**ΔRecall 六格全部 ≥0（召回增益跨数据集同向）而 ΔF1 在 sick×smote 翻负**（橙色柱全员非负 vs 蓝色柱 sick 侧贴零/翻负）；sick×cost 两根柱同时贴零（恒等映射，§D6 发现 2）。

**图注定稿：**

> **Figure D2.** Effect of each imbalance-handling strategy (SMOTE, cost-sensitive learning, threshold moving) relative to plain training, averaged over the four base models (DT, RF, LR, SVC) on the 10-fold cross-validation protocol: ΔF1 (blue) and ΔRecall (orange), Δ = strategy − plain, from the twelve pre-specified paired comparisons (Nadeau–Bengio corrected t-tests). Left: credit-g (positive class = bad, 30.0% positive, neg:pos 2.3:1); right: sick (positive class = sick, 6.1% positive, neg:pos 15.3:1). Recall gains are non-negative in every setting, while F1 gains flip sign on the more imbalanced sick dataset — the strategies reliably buy recall, but the precision cost depends on the dataset.

## 3. 图 D3 `fig_d_confusion` — 冠军混淆矩阵（counts + 归一化双视图）

**取数**：`results/all_metrics_v1.csv`（D4 汇池 TN/FP/FN/TP，cv10），`figures/data/fig_d_confusion.csv`。

| 冠军 | TN | FP | FN | TP | 行数 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| creditg_lr_cost_v1 | 391 | 309 | 41 | 259 | 预测 good 700 / bad 300 |
| sick_rf_smote_v1 | 3,520 | 21 | 26 | 205 | 预测 negative 3,541 / sick 231 |

行=预测、列=真实（(+)=正类）；右列按**预测行**归一化（每行合计 100%），与左列同色梯。credit-g 冠军的代价结构一目了然：300 个 bad 里 259 个被抓到（recall 0.863），但 700 个预测 bad 里 41 个误伤 good（precision 259/300=0.863）；sick 冠军两端都干净（FP 21、FN 26）。

**图注定稿：**

> **Figure D3.** Confusion matrices of the per-dataset F1 champions on the 10-fold cross-validation protocol (pooled out-of-fold predictions; rows = predicted class, columns = actual class; (+) marks the positive class): credit-g champion lr_cost with positive class bad (top) and sick champion rf_smote with positive class sick (bottom). Left column: absolute counts; right column: row-normalized shares (each predicted class sums to 100%). Cell shading uses a single-hue sequential blue ramp; numbers are exact values.
