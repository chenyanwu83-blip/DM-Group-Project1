## 一、讲稿正文（英文 · 2′30″ · 约 360 词 · 口语版）

### [0:00–0:30 · Slide B1 · sick 是什么]
> So let me introduce our second dataset — **sick**, from the UCI Thyroid
> Disease collection. It has **3,772 patient records and 30 attributes**.
> And right away, you can see the problem on this slide: the classes are
> heavily imbalanced — **3,541 negative, only 231 sick**. That's roughly
> **94 to 6**. Two quick notes on wording, because we want to be precise:
> "sick" here means *sick-euthyroid* — thyroid disease with normal hormone
> levels — **not** hypothyroidism. And "negative" just means the screening
> found nothing — it does **not** mean perfectly healthy. We treat "sick"
> as the positive class in every metric from here on.

【讲法：开场上来就指着幻灯片的条形图说"you can see the problem"；两个 not 重读；语速正常】

### [0:30–1:30 · Slide B2 · 缺失：我发现了什么（本段是灵魂）]
> Now, when I first opened this dataset, it looked really bad: **every
> single row had missing values**. But once I dug into *why*, the picture
> completely changed — and this is the part I want you to remember.
> Each lab test comes as a pair: a flag saying whether the test was done,
> and the value itself. If the flag says *false*, the value is missing —
> because **the test was simply never done**. It's not lost data; it's
> data that never existed. The clearest case is **TBG — 100% missing in
> all 3,772 rows**, because that test was never done at all. I checked
> all six lab pairs: **zero contradictions**. So this dataset is
> incomplete, but it's honest.
>
> And that changes how you handle it. You cannot impute TBG — there is
> nothing to impute from — so we **remove** it. For the rest, about 30%
> of rows still have gaps. Should we just delete those rows? I tested it:
> you would throw away **30% of the patients — including 19 real sick
> patients — and the F1 does not improve**. Not worth it. So we **fill the
> gaps instead** — means for numbers, most-frequent values for categories.

【讲法：本段自带故事线（先吓一跳→发现机制→改变处理），按箭头推进；"never done / never existed / honest" 三处放慢；"Not worth it" 停半拍再说，是全场最口语的一句】

### [1:30–2:10 · Slide B3 · 在哪里补，和补什么一样重要（防泄漏）]
> Here's the detail people often miss: **where** you fill the gaps matters
> just as much as how. Everything — **imputation, encoding, scaling, and
> SMOTE — lives inside the pipeline**. So it is fitted only on the
> training folds, and never on the data being tested. On top of that, the
> whole team shares the **same locked splits**, which means every number
> you see today is directly comparable. And we built in a safety check:
> an equal-cost setting returns exactly the plain baseline — if those two
> ever disagree, something is broken.

【讲法：第一句是观点句，抬头看观众说；"inside the pipeline" 指着幻灯片流水线图；最后一句自信收】

### [2:10–2:30 · 集成彩蛋 + 交棒]
> One last thing — a nice surprise from the ensemble study I ran:
> **AdaBoost plus SMOTE reached an F1 of 0.937**, beating our best
> main-matrix model — re-weighting hard examples worked even better than
> re-sampling. **C** will now take you through the models.

【讲法：彩蛋语气（a nice surprise）；交棒词按当天实际顺序改；整段 20 秒，别恋战】

## 三、预期问答（V5.0 要求：2+1 且**含 1 道 bagging/boosting 题**）

1. **【bagging/boosting · 必备】Why does SMOTE destroy logistic regression's precision but not the random forest's?**
   → SMOTE 在 one-hot 空间插值造出"混合类别"样本，扭曲线性边界（LR 0.79→0.37）；RF 的 bagging+随机子空间让多数投票稀释合成噪声（0.94→0.91）。集成研究补充：AdaBoost 同样保持精度（重加权修正假阳性）、纯 bagging 不保持——机制是"去相关+重加权"，不是集成本身。
2. **Why not drop the 61 duplicate rows?**
   → 对齐 UCI 官方 3,772 口径（删了全组哈希与切分锚点作废）；已在报告 ch3 披露为数据质量问题。
3. **整体题：Why these two datasets?**
   → 五维对比（规模 1,000 vs 3,772 / 不平衡 70:30 vs 94:6 / 缺失 0 vs 5.4% / 属性构成 / 领域），支撑 RQ1–4；集成研究再贡献一次"赢家翻转"（ada 在 sick 登顶、credit-g 不复现）。

**加练（时间富余再背）**：*What if SMOTE were outside the CV loop?* → 合成样本泄入验证折、指标虚高；我们的 SMOTE 全在 imblearn Pipeline 折内合成（C10 复核记录可查）。
